# P07-T05 — Delegated administration and Role-creation boundaries

Status: implemented on `phase-07-authorization-delegation`.

## Delivered

- `assertCanDelegate` now requires `manage_access` on the target scope for every grant, deny, and revoke; a grant also requires the actor to possess the capability being delegated. Revoke removes a direct rule and never creates a replacement grant.
- Role creation and configuration are evaluator-backed: Domain owners/platform administrators can operate across their Domain, while a scoped `manage_roles` holder is limited to the covered Department. Ordinary rank, `assign_subordinates`, or Department participation does not imply Role creation.
- `assign_subordinates` is evaluated against active Role assignments and the same-Department ancestry graph. Self, peer, ancestor, cross-Department, inactive, and forged targets fail closed.
- Role assignment checkboxes now use server-computed `canAssignRole` decisions and are disabled when the current actor cannot assign/remove that Role. Folder access remains a separate control and payload.
- Folder management mutations honor nested Folder authority and write through the existing Domain audit seam. Permission-rule mutations carry active Character context and support the frozen Character/User/Role plus Folder/Document polymorphic boundary.
- Management navigation and page gates are capability-driven, while the primary Domain navigation remains stable and no administration mode or redundant Domain selector is introduced.
- Document creation now validates the authoring context: ordinary members must use an active controlled Character in the Domain; only an owner, operational Domain admin, or platform admin may create without a Character.
